\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

This section surveys relevant prior research and highlights how \sister{} differs from existing literature.

\subsection{Foundational Techniques}
Early methods relied on traditional statistical modeling \cite{knuth1984texbook}.
While foundational, these methods lack adaptation mechanisms under non-stationary distributions.

\subsection{Modern Approaches}
Modern computational paradigms leverage deep learning architectures and distributed synchronization \cite{vaswani2017attention}.
However, prior studies often neglect trade-offs between efficiency and robustness.
In contrast, \sister{} explicitly optimizes for these trade-offs.
